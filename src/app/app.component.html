Frequently Asked Questions

How does PDF Compare handle label variations?

Our normalization engine recognizes synonyms like "Invoice Number" vs "Invoice No.", aligning them as the same entity while reporting the cosmetic label variation separately from numerical values.

Are my uploaded documents secure and private?

Yes. Text extraction and thumbnail generation happen 100% in your local browser sandbox. No sensitive document data is stored or logged to external servers.

What is the difference between Free and Pro plans?

The Free plan allows instant pairwise 2-file comparison with full table/JSON/summary exports. Pro unlocks multi-document diffing (3+ files at once), batch processing, and priority API rates.

Can I compare scanned or OCR documents?

Yes! The engine parses OCR text layers and flags extraction warnings if character streams are unreadable or incomplete.
