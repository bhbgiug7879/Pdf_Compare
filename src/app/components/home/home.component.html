Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how our privacy-first browser PDF engine processes your documents safely.

Are my documents stored or saved on your servers?

No. Our core suite uses client-side WebAssembly (WASM) and HTML5 Canvas technologies to parse and modify PDFs directly in your web browser memory. Your files are never stored or uploaded to external storage disks.

Is this PDF tool suite completely free to use?

Yes! You can merge, split, compress, edit, convert, and sign PDF documents as a guest with zero mandatory account sign-up requirements.

Does this work on mobile devices and iPads?

Absolutely. Our responsive layout and tap-to-upload interface are optimized for iOS, iPadOS, Android, and all modern mobile web browsers.

How does the AI PDF Compare tool work?

Our AI diff engine parses text coordinates, clause hierarchies, and table cells between two PDF documents to compute additions, deletions, and value modifications with redline highlights.
